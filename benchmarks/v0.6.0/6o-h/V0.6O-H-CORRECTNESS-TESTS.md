## 2. The 6O-F reproductions

On `f9a364d` the 8 gated reproductions failed in 3 runs out of 3 (`V0.6O-H-BASELINE.md` §2, `raw/h0-baseline/`). On 6O-H the gate is removed and **all 21 tests in the file pass** in both fan-out modes:
- counter after its deadline, with `no`, `everysec` and `always`;
- `INCR` with `EXPIRE` once;
- `SET … KEEPTTL`;
- a replica applying late;
- `APPEND`, `HINCRBY` and `RPUSH`;
- `LSET`.
