## 3. End to end against Redis 7.2.5

`tools/expiry_restart_diff.py` runs 20 timelines on real processes (`appendfsync always`, SIGKILL, restarts after the deadlines):
- **`f9a364d` differs from Redis in 10 of the 20** (`raw/diff/restart-baseline.md`; the same 10 again in `restart-final.md`). It resurrects counters (also across `RENAME`), a counter in `MULTI`, `APPEND`, `HINCRBY` and `LPUSH` written while live, and the snapshot-plus-AOF and multiple-restart timelines. It **refuses to restart** after `LSET` or `RENAME` of a volatile key.
- A replica (`tools/replica_diff.py`) paused past the deadlines, then promoted and restarted from its own files, resurrects `APPEND`'s value and loses an extended deadline.

Full tables: `V0.6O-H-CORRECTNESS-TESTS.md` §3, raw under `raw/diff/`.
