## Per PR

### 0a: DEDUP acknowledgement follows the AOF stage (#128)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH NITS.
- **Confirmed sound:**
  - Every primary-side path that makes a mark or refusal visible is now ordered after its AOF stage. That covers TCP, WS, the capacity path, no-op outcomes, refusals and WS early replay. EXEC cannot create marks.
  - `always` fail-stops without acknowledgement, and `everysec`/`no` stay non-fatal.
  - No lock-order inversion.
- **Low, perf, accepted (documented in the PR):** every TCP DEDUP write now holds the process-wide `dedup_order` across its own append, so DEDUP writes are serialized server-wide. That was already true of WS DEDUP writes and replicated primaries.
- **Low, deferred to 0b and 0d:** WS refusals outside `dedup_order`, and replica-side ordering.
- **Nits, fixed:** the test hook's count is reset per arming, and its worker-blocking caveat is documented.

### 0c: QSUB `qstate` follows the AOF stage (#129)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH NITS.
- **Confirmed sound:**
  - The tail covers every earlier deferred write: batches are performed in queue order, including the `busy`-before-`complete` handoff.
  - Deferral always happens under stripes.
  - No lock is held across the await, and the wait is cancellation-safe.
  - `qstate` is still sent before any keychange.
- **Low, fixed:** the `always` failure test could not tell a close from a hang.
- **Low, documented:** holding the tail skews the coalescer's statistics, which affects heuristics only.
- **Info, accepted contract (6N-B D5/O2):** a plain `GET` can read a deferred write before its `write(2)`. Only `qstate` is gated.

### 0d: replica-side DEDUP ordering (#130)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH NITS.
- **Confirmed sound:**
  - `lock_commands_and_keys(&[], once(client))` takes a real stripe.
  - The `handle_replica` (full and partial) and promotion barriers exclude it.
  - No new lock hazard.
- **F1, Low–Medium, older than this PR, fixed:** a full resync installed the DEDUP table outside the data's barrier.
- **N1, fixed:** the race test could not reproduce the losing interleaving.
- **Pre-existing, recorded:** a chained replica's full resync does not propagate the replaced keyspace to its existing sub-replicas or to its own AOF. That is outside DEDUP ordering.

### 0b: refused WS commands take no global barrier (#132)
**Round 1: CHANGES REQUIRED.**
- **High, fixed in `7bde58f`:** the keys-scope refusal branch still took the refused command's stripes. A refused `DEDUP c n DEL k1 … k2000` took nearly every stripe with the gate closed: the same barrier as RV-6.
- **Confirmed sound in the deviation from 6N-B:** a lock shared with SAVE would not have excluded `handle_replica`'s cut. One identity stripe excludes every all-stripe barrier.
- **Low, fixed:** the per-connection pace could outlast the token.
- **Low, fixed by 0d:** replica apply of refusal records.

**Round 2: APPROVE WITH NITS.** The High is closed, and no other path records a refusal under a refused command's stripes.

### 0e: unauthenticated WS sockets and the fast path (#131)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH NITS.
- **Confirmed sound:**
  - Fast-path soundness, including the borrowed path, FLUSHDB and channel-only tokens.
  - Nothing else reads the count.
  - No counter leak or double count, including cancellation during the gate close.
  - No weakening of authorization.
- **Low, documented:** an open server *without* a password authenticates every socket at connect, so those sockets are still counted. That is consistent with what they may see.
- **Low, accepted:** a credentialed client looping connect and AUTH closes the gate once per round. Each close is bounded and needs a credential, and the gate used to close on every connect.
- **Nits, fixed:** a token-auth and token-expiry test, and the router doc.

### Combined: the five fixes at `d0b9043`
**Verdict:** PASS WITH CONDITIONS. All of the following were checked across the full set:
- the complete lock-order table;
- starvation;
- acknowledgement order;
- the seven combined scenarios the brief names.

Results:
- **No lock cycle.** `dedup_order` is only ever taken before any stripe, stripes are taken in ascending order, and no std mutex is held across an await.
- **No acknowledgement observable before its AOF stage.**
