## 1. How a group becomes visible

1. Every member is parsed and checked first (normalized to a push, deadline clamp, no trailing bytes, no DEDUP record).
2. One `lock_commands` over all members (the replication-order stripes), then `still_following` once: promotion's all-write barrier waits for the whole group (`promotion_waits_for_a_group_being_applied`, kills mutant M14).
3. `KeyValueStore::apply_transaction(&commands, clock_ms(), …)` — the primary's own 6O-G boundary: the union of the members' store stripes held and marked `SEQ_MULTI` from the first member to the last; inside `without_expiry` for an `expirylog1` stream, at one frozen instant otherwise. A replica's store is a follower, so only its keys' stripes are held. No second locking implementation.
4. Effects after the apply: one group into this node's AOF and to its sub-replicas (`on_group_logged`: the frames move into `fan_out_group`, no extra copy), then WATCH/QSUB notifications through `NotificationPlan` exactly as `EXEC`'s (each key once, at its last change; a full value when changed more than once; `removed_after_set`; `FLUSHDB`), then sync pushes.
5. The resume point becomes the group's last offset; the acknowledgement follows the existing rule.

## 4. Failure

| Case | Behaviour | Test |
|---|---|---|
| a member returns an error (the replica diverged) | the rest are skipped; with an AOF, persistence latched (`MISCONF` until a checkpoint); stream reset, sync clients resynchronized, watchers told; protocol error → full sync | `a_member_that_fails_stops_the_group_and_forces_a_full_sync`, `a_failed_group_with_an_aof_latches_and_keeps_its_prefix_until_the_full_sync` |
| a member panics | contained by `apply_transaction` (metadata repaired); the same path; nothing of the group logged | `a_member_that_panics_is_contained_and_forces_a_full_sync` |
| a failure on a middle replica promoted before it reconnects | its stream was reset under the group's stripes: the sub-replica takes a full sync of the actual keyspace | `a_failed_group_on_a_middle_replica_resets_its_sub_replicas` (review C M1 / review D D-1) |
| disconnect, promotion or shutdown while assembling | nothing of the group applied; resume point before it | `V0.6O-I-REPLICATION-PROTOCOL.md` §4 |

**Readers can see a part-applied group on this error path only, until the forced full sync installs** (the store cannot undo a member that ran), as on the primary in 6O-G4.

The effects step is not cancellation-safe (a drop after the apply leaves the resume point before the group); no caller drops it, and the single-frame path is the same (review C L3).

## 5. Cost

Reader stalls while a group applies are the primary's 6O-G trade-off, now on replicas: a large group blocks readers of its keys for its apply time. Measured in `V0.6O-I-PERFORMANCE.md`.

## 6. Mutants

Killed: per-member apply (M5), a half-applied group kept after a failure (M13), no order stripes (M14, by the promotion test), a group relayed before its apply succeeded (M18), per-member notifications after the group (A7, the pre-H1 behaviour).
