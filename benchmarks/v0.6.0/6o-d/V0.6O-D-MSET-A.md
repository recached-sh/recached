## Compatibility with the D1 atomicity fix

The two can be combined; this is checked against the reviewed design, not implemented.

- **MSET's stripe hold.** The design holds all of MSET's write-order stripes for the whole command (`order_keys` before the first pair, released after the last). D1's marks live in exactly that hold: `order_keys` marks every stripe `ACTIVE` and multi-step before the first mutation, and `StripeHold::drop` advances both words after the last. MSET-A's `run_probed_mset` would call `order_keys(keys, true)` (a multi-key write) and keep the guard alive across its pairs, so readers see it whole without further change.
- **Duplicate keys.** `MSET k 1 k 2` stays a multi-step write (`several_steps`, more than one key before deduplication). D1's single-key post-validation hides the in-between pair from readers. The design's per-pair probes and telescoping accounting are unaffected.
- **Capacity.** The design's fast path is limited to `!enforce`, and D1 adds nothing there. The capped path, with its rollback mark, is unchanged by both.
- **Owned pairs.** Owned-value moves on AOF replay and replica apply are unaffected by D1, which touches only the stripes and the read side.
