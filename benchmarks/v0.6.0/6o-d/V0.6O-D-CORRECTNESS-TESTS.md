All results are on the final atomicity code: #165, code-identical to `1965943`. Raw output is in `raw/d1-suites/`, `raw/d0-reproduction/` and `raw/mutation-d1/`.

## 1. Deterministic interleavings

**The hook.** `store::write_pause` is test-only (`cfg(test)`, or the `test-hooks` feature enabled only by the server's dev-dependency). It is scoped to one store. It can stop:
- a write after it mutates a chosen key: inside MSET, DEL and RENAME, and after a general write's `exec` (before emptied-collection removal and rollback);
- a reader after its validation load, after its read (single-key), or after an MGET's or EXISTS's first key.

Every wait is a condition with a 20 s bound; nothing relies on sleeps. Histories are recorded against one clock and decided by an exhaustive linearizability check (`linearization`), which has its own unit test.

## 5. Mutation testing

`mutation/mutate-6od.py` applies each mutant to a scratch copy of `store.rs` and runs the store guards (`atomicity_tests`), then the TCP guards (`mset_atomicity`) when those survive.

| Run | Code | Killed | Survived |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | first implementation | 12/15 | M3 (an anchor bug in the script), M9, M13 (test gaps) |
| 2 | tests strengthened | 15/15 | — |
| 3 | review round 1 | 21/22 | M22 (EXISTS read as its first key) |
| 4 | review round 2 | 26/28 | M22, M25 (release without advancing the epoch) |
| **5** | **final** | **28/28** | — |

The mutants, by the brief's list:
- **Reader does not acquire or validate:**
  - M1: no wait;
  - M10, M11, M13, M15, M20: each entry point bypassed;
  - M12: borrowed MGET bypassed;
  - M18: set operations bypassed;
  - M22: EXISTS read as its first key;
  - M23: single-key read skips its post-validation.
- **Writer releases protection early, or never marks:**
  - M5: marks only the first stripe;
  - M6: not marked multi-key;
  - M7: no marks at all;
  - M16: a key named twice not marked;
  - M17: a rollback not marked;
  - M21: an emptying pop not marked;
  - M24: the multi word never marked;
  - M25: release without advancing the epoch.
- **Inconsistent stripe order:** M4, the fallback descending (killed by the deadlock watchdog).
- **Version validation skipped:** M3 (multi-key), M8 (release without advancing the version), M23.
- **Accepting a mixed snapshot:** M2 (validates but ignores writes in progress), M14 and M26 (a retry keeps a partial reply).
- **Other:** M9 (MGET reads the clock per key), M19 (DEDUP), M27 (HINCRBYFLOAT creates the hash first), M28 (an empty push creates the list).

MSET-A and MSET-B mutants are **inapplicable**: those stages are not implemented.
