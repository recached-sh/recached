## 3. Profiling tools

The host has no `perf`, `strace` or `bpftrace`. The Flatpak SDK's `perf` 7.3.0 runs natively on the host through the runtime's own loader (`hostperf.sh`). With `perf_event_paranoid = 2`, user-space events work: `perf stat -p` counts `instructions:u`, `cycles:u`, `cache-misses:u` and `branch-misses:u`, and `perf record -e cycles:u -g --user-callchains` records frame-pointer call graphs. DWARF and LBR unwinding are refused, hence the `-fp` builds. Context switches come from `/proc/<pid>/status`; Tokio's task count is not observable without `tokio_unstable` (a limitation).
