Each reviewer was an independent agent that had not written the tooling, the analysis or px. It got the code (the H, I, P and px trees), the tools and the raw data, ran nothing but read-only analysis while campaigns occupied the host, and wrote its review into `raw/reviews/`. ## Review D, round 1 (`raw/reviews/REVIEW-D-round1.md`)

Supported verdict: **PASS WITH CONDITIONS**, provisional. No BLOCKED criterion is met; "repeated full sync below sustainable load" holds for the product (H and I as well as P at 1,380 tx/s after a forced resync), so it is an operating limit predating 6O-I, not a 6O-I regression. FIX REQUIRED is not supported: no production fix is needed before v0.6.0; px is optional and not yet approvable as written. Classification: the CPU/instruction and RSS costs of the 10,000-write cell are a 6O-I regression with a proven focused repair (px), not a cost of atomicity; about half of its transaction loss is the replica sharing a CPU (a consequence of applying a group at once); the stream-lock hold is an expected cost of atomicity; the replica findings are a 6O-H design limit, the same in every build.
