## 3. The benchmarks' own checks

Every benchmark run checks the state it leaves (methodology §2–§3), and these are correctness evidence in their own right:

- **Paced replica runs (`r1`, `r2`, …):** the primary's keyspace equals the expected one computed from the acknowledged transactions, value for value; the replica's equals the primary's (digest of every key and value, `DBSIZE`, TTL count); the replica restarted alone from its own AOF equals it again. Every check that completed passed, at every rate, including the overloaded runs with 13–23 full syncs: no divergence, no partially applied transaction, nothing lost across a crash-restart from the replica's AOF where the restart was checked. Exceptions, below: the restart check did not complete in `r4`, the tmpfs-quota runs and P's `r6`, and P's `r6` verification was lost with its run record (it ended at lag 0).
- **Contention runs:** after the drain, the replica's digest equals the primary's in every run of every build.
- **Restart from the replica's own AOF** matched the expected keyspace in every run where it completed: the ladder and refinement (`r1`–`r3`), and after the forced resynchronizations of `r5` (6 of 6, AOF 0.6–1.5 GB). It did **not** complete where the replica's AOF had grown for a long time: the 30-minute soaks (`r4`: 23–25 GB, past the driver's wait) and P's rerun (`r6`: about 11 GB, **OOM-killed at 12.3 GB RSS**). Cause: a replica never autosaves (replicated writes do not mark the store dirty), and AOF replay reads the whole file into memory; both pre-existing and the same in H and P. A resource-safety risk for the release, not a correctness defect of 6O-I (`V0.6O-I8-RELEASE-GATE.md` §5).

## 4. The CI flake (6O-I #200's first CI run)

`tests::replica_expirylog_6oh::a_replica_reads_ahead_while_its_full_sync_checkpoint_runs` failed with "the replica never caught up" on #200's first run (976574a, Load & Chaos job), together with a timeout in `tests::coalesced_fanout::pipelined_writers_on_four_workers_strand_nothing`; both passed on rerun.

Reading the test: it charges every streamed frame a 1 ms tokio sleep (`test_apply_delay_us = 1000`) and the checkpoint a 5 s sleep, writes 3,072 + 2,048 frames, then waits for the last key with a fixed polling budget (3,000 × 10 ms). Its assertions on disconnects (`queue_disconnects() == 0`) and synchronizations (`syncs() == (0, 1)`) come *before* the catch-up assertion and passed: there was no disconnect and no extra full sync. The replica was merely slower than the budget. The test writes single `SET`s only, a path #200 does not change.

**Reproduction attempts** (`raw/tests/{p,h}-flaky-*.txt`, `*-chaos-2cpu-*.txt`):

| | P (`54ff38a`) | H (`6068337`) |
|---|---|---|
| the test alone, 10 runs | **10 passed** (16.8–18.1 s) | **10 passed** (16.8–18.1 s) |
| the test starved: pinned to 2 CPUs with 4 busy loops on the same 2, 10 runs | **10 passed** (24.7–26.3 s) | **10 passed** (24.7–24.8 s) |
| the whole load-and-chaos suite on 2 CPUs, 3 runs | **865/865 passed** ×3 (147 s each) | **812/812 passed** ×3 (144 s each) |

The flake did not reproduce: 0 failures in 40 isolated runs, and the test (and `pipelined_writers_on_four_workers_strand_nothing`) passed in all 8 suite runs that include it, 6 of them on 2 CPUs.

**Conclusion: a timing budget on an oversubscribed CI runner, not a product defect, and not specific to 6O-I.** The last wait polls at least 3,000 × 10 ms = 30 s of wall time, while the whole test takes 17 s alone and 25 s starved here; to fail it, the replica must apply the second burst (2,048 frames, each delayed by a 1 ms tokio sleep) more than about 6× slower than under this host's starvation. The same CI job's simultaneous timeout in `pipelined_writers_on_four_workers_strand_nothing` points to a starved runner at that moment. The assertions that would reveal a real failure, no disconnect and exactly one full sync, passed on CI. H and P behave identically. **Recommended (test-only, not made in this phase):** wait on an explicit deadline (for example 120 s, measured with `Instant`) instead of an iteration count, so a slow runner gets the time the test means to give.
