**The one failure** is a pre-existing, load-sensitive flake, recorded in 6O-F. The test sets `SET k v PX 5` then `WATCH k`. On a loaded host the key can expire before `WATCH`, and an absent key's later expiry is correctly not a change. It failed only in full-suite runs while two reviewer builds or mutation runs loaded the 6-core host. In isolation on an idle host it failed **0 of 20** times on the baseline `25948a1` and **0 of 20** on `47e6442` (`raw/tests/watch-expiry-flake.txt`). It touches no EXEC path. CI runs the suite on idle runners.
