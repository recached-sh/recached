# v0.6.0 Phase 6N-C5: correctness tests

## Scope

Two production changes need tests:
- **PR 2:** the replica sender holds its flush until the end of the scheduling round.
- **PR 3:** a coalesced writer does not yield while another performer is writing.

PR 1 adds INFO counters only. No ordering, offset, backlog, AOF, DEDUP, promotion or security code changed in this phase.

## New tests

### PR 2: `server-native/src/tests/repl_sender_batching.rs`

These tests drive the real sender (`handle_replica`) over TCP against a scripted replica that speaks the handshake.

| Test | Runtime | What it checks |
|---|---|---|
| `a_round_of_writers_shares_one_flush` | 1 worker | 32 writers × 4 rounds, each frame fanned out then yielded. Offsets are contiguous, each writer's frames stay in order, at least 8 frames go per flush, and flushes are held to the round. |
| `a_burst_larger_than_one_flush_is_capped_and_ordered` | 1 worker | 2000 frames queued before the sender runs. Every one arrives in order. There are at least ⌈2000/512⌉ flushes, so the cap holds. At most one flush is held. The frame count is exact. |
| `concurrent_writers_stream_exactly_the_backlog` | **4 workers** | 8 writers × 400 frames. The live stream is contiguous, nothing is lost or duplicated, and per-writer order holds. A second replica resuming from offset 0 gets **byte-identical** frames from the backlog. |
| `a_stalled_replica_is_dropped_and_resumes_from_the_backlog` | 2 workers | A replica that stops reading is disconnected while writers never stall (asserted under 10 s). It then resumes from its offset, receives exactly the missing range from the backlog, and the live stream continues contiguously. |
| `aborting_the_sender_before_its_round_flush_strands_nothing` | 1 worker | The sender is aborted while holding a flush. Its channel is already drained, so no byte charge stays accounted. Nothing reaches the replica after the abort, and the next write unregisters the dead replica. |
| `a_small_channel_is_drained_on_every_wake` | 1 worker | A 16-frame channel and 128 frames from 32 writers. The replica is not disconnected. |
| `one_round_of_pipelines_is_drained_as_it_is_queued` | 1 worker | 40 × 128-frame pipelines (5120 frames) into a 4096-frame channel. No disconnect, and the rounds are batched. |
| `large_frames_do_not_overflow_a_batching_sender` | 1 worker | 32 × 2 frames of 32 KiB against a 1 MiB byte budget. No disconnect. |
| `acknowledgements_are_still_read` | 2 workers | Lag goes 100 → 0 once the replica acknowledges. |
| `pipelined_rounds_at_default_capacity_with_acks` | 1 worker | `#[ignore]`, a **measurement**. It checks that what the replica received is contiguous, and prints whether one round overran the channel ([the recorded exposure](V0.6N-C5-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md#the-recorded-exposure-r1-residual)). |

### PR 3: `server-native/src/tests/coalesced_fanout.rs`

| Test | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `a_writer_does_not_yield_while_a_performer_is_busy` | A performer is held mid-batch (the `AofHold` hook). A second writer's first poll reaches `lead()` (`lead_busy` +1) and parks on its batch (`gate_parked` +1), which a yielding writer cannot do. It makes no gain or miss observation, even after completion. Its frame is written once, after the held one. |
| `frames_queued_behind_a_busy_performer_are_all_swept` | 8 writers queue behind a held performer; 4 are cancelled after queueing. All 8 frames land once, in queue order, the queue ends empty, and no writer yielded. |
