## RSS

The verdict's informational line reads "16.2% growth over the second half". That compares against the **final** sample, which fell on a replica restart (298.5 MB). The 5-second samples (`samples.csv`) show:

| Window | Primary RSS |
|---|---|
| 3 min to 31 min | Flat at 254–257 MB |
| 31 min onwards | Steps to about 267 MB and stays there |
| Transient peaks (281, 293, 298 MB) | Only at forced resyncs (`stall:yes`) and replica restarts, each back to about 267 MB within one sample |

Steady-state growth from minute 3 to the end is about +4%. The run shows stability over 40 minutes. It does **not** prove a long-run plateau; the open 2G memory-plateau question is unaffected.

## Scope

The primary has a replication listener, so its writes took the per-command AOF append, not the coalescing path ([Test B](V0.6N-1-REPLICATION-AOF-PROFILE.md)). This soak verifies the correctness of the integrated production topology. It does **not** demonstrate the coalescing speed-up.
