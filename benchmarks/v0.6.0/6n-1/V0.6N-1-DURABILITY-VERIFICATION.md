Exit 74 (`EX_IOERR`) is documented in `docs/server/configuration.md`.

- Every `always_*` subprocess case asserts the exit.

- `always_failure_in_the_shutdown_checkpoint_exits_as_a_fail_stop`
- `an_always_truncation_failure_with_appends_in_its_batch_stops`
- Negative controls:
  - `an_always_truncation_failure_alone_does_not_stop`
  - `everysec_failure_keeps_its_contract_and_does_not_stop`
  - `everysec_failures_still_pass_replies_and_do_not_stop`
  - `always_short_write_is_completed_and_acknowledged`
